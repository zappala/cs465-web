# Final Project

For the final project, the goal is for you to explore the capabilities of AI systems to do the work for this class. You should spend 10 to 15 hours with an AI coding environment to do the exercises in this class and, time provided, to push beyond the class. I highly recommend purchasing a Claude Pro account for one month, but you are welcome to use other AI coding tools.

For this assignment only, you are at level 4 of the BYU CS [AI use guidelines](https://claude.ai/public/artifacts/b1e35a55-f03f-4120-ba4f-b83a1ee3fc83).

**Do not use AI to hack any system. You do not have authorization for this.**

Some examples of activities that are encouraged:

- Complete assignments from the class.
- Do CTF challenges from the class, provided you are not pointing the AI at the CTF servers, but instead having it write code on your computer that you then upload to the CTF server.
- Create new assignments for the class, and then complete those assignments.
- Push into an area we have not covered, such as [Reverse Engineering](https://pwn.college/intro-to-cybersecurity/reverse-engineering/) or [Return Oriented Programming](https://pwn.college/program-security/return-oriented-programming/).

## Deliverables

Turn in:

- A report covering (a) what you have done with AI as a partner, (b) what you have learned, and (c) an overall assessment of AI's capabilities for these tasks. I expect a minimum of 2 pages, but be as long as you want.

## Grading

- This will be graded on how well you engage with this topic and how extensively you are able to use AI. Start early, don't postpone to the last week of class.
