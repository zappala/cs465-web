# Homework: Reflections on Trusting Trust

<UsedIn>[Fall 2018](/fall2018) to [Winter 2020](/winter2020), [Winter 2022](/winter2022) to [Fall 2023](/fall2023) (as Homework 12); [Fall 2020](/fall2020) to [Fall 2021](/fall2021) (as Homework 11)</UsedIn>


Read the classic Ken Thompson article [Reflections on Trusting Trust](https://www.archive.ece.cmu.edu/~ganger/712.fall02/papers/p761-thompson.pdf). Answer the following questions:

1. Describe briefly and clearly how the attack on the compiler works.

1. If you suspect that your machine has been compromised, what should
   you do about it?

1.  What other kinds of software like compilers do we usually trust
that have the potential to be compromised?
