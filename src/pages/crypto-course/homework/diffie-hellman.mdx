# Homework: Diffie-Hellman

<UsedIn>[Fall 2018](/fall2018) to [Fall 2023](/fall2023) (as Homework 5)</UsedIn>


1. Describe the Diffie-Hellman protocol.

1. Show how Mallory can conduct a man-in-the-middle attack when Alice
and Bob perform the DH protocol from question #1.

1. What is the recommended key size for the prime modulus p in DH?

1. Why is the recommended size for p for DH so much larger than the
recommended key size for AES?

(search external sources for answering the last two questions, and
cite your source)

on the due date.
